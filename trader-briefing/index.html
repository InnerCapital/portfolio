On-Site Screen Operation
Reference only - not pre-event reading ▼
1
Power On
Rear bottom-left button - hold 2 sec until indicator turns white. ~10 sec startup before content loads.
2
Verify & Adjust Brightness
Check content is correct and undistorted. Brightness: MENU → Picture → Brightness. Outdoor 80–100%, Indoor 60–80%. If image wrong - contact Acme Hire, do not adjust source settings.
3
Power Off
Hold power 3 sec. Do not unplug from mains unless directed by Acme Hire.
4
Screen Blank / Frozen → USB Fallback
Hard restart: hold power 6 sec, wait 30 sec. If still blank: find AH SCREENS USB stick in unit, insert into screen, press INPUT/SOURCE on remote, select USB. When connectivity restored, switch back to CMS. Do not delete files. Contact hello@acmehire.example if stick is missing.

Compliance & Handover
Your Responsibility
Insurance — due 7 days before delivery

You must provide valid certificates of insurance before delivery. Minimum cover required:

  • Public Liability — £10,000,000 minimum
  • Products Liability — £5,000,000 minimum
  • Employers Liability — required if you employ staff

Acme Hire reserves the right to refuse delivery or cancel the booking if valid certificates are not provided at least 7 days before delivery. Delivery is not confirmed until proof of insurance has been received.

Site Paperwork (RAMS & Access Permits)

Some sites need a Risk Assessment and Method Statement (RAMS) or an access permit. If yours does, we'll tell you in writing at least 7 days before delivery.

Send us any site information we ask for by the same date. If you book less than 7 days before delivery, send it within 24 hours of booking.

Asset ID — Check on Delivery

When your unit arrives, check the Asset ID on the unit against the Asset ID on your Hire Agreement before you sign the Handover Certificate.

If they do not match and you were not notified of a unit swap, do not sign — contact your Acme Hire representative first.

CCTV

You must not access, copy, or tamper with CCTV footage or hardware. Acme Hire manages this remotely for asset security purposes.

Confidentiality & PR

You must not make public statements about your relationship with Acme Hire or disclose confidential information without prior written consent. This includes social media posts that reference Acme Hire by name or implication.

Acme Hire Is a Supplier - Not an Operator

All trading compliance is the Customer's responsibility, including anyone trading from your units. Acme Hire's responsibility ends at the fabric, fit-out and electrical installation of the unit. Everything beyond that is yours.

Damage Deposit — due 7 days before delivery

A Damage Deposit of £1,000 per unit is payable no later than 7 days before delivery. Your deposit, insurance certificates and signed declaration are all due by the same date. We can't confirm your delivery until we have all three.

The deposit is refunded within 14 days of collection, less any deductions for damage beyond fair wear and tear, additional cleaning, or other costs due under the Hire Agreement.

If the unit is not returned cleaned to the standard it was received in, a standard cleaning charge of £250 is deducted from your deposit. Any other damage is charged at cost — see your Hire Agreement for full terms.

Take your own photos on arrival and again before you leave, after cleaning, as your own evidence of condition. Any damage or condition issue not reported within 24 hours will be treated as accepted.

PLEASE READ CAREFULLY BEFORE SIGNING By signing this declaration, I, the undersigned, on behalf of the Customer named on the Hire Agreement, confirm and agree to the following:

1. Infrastructure Only. I acknowledge that Acme Hire is a supplier of commercial kitchen infrastructure only and not a trading operator. Acme Hire's responsibility is limited to the physical fabric, fit-out and electrical installation of the unit up to the distribution board.

2. Handover and Condition. I will inspect the unit(s) on delivery, check the Asset ID matches the Hire Agreement, and record their condition on the Handover and Commissioning Certificate. Any defects not noted on that certificate must be reported in writing within 24 hours of delivery. Responsibility for the unit(s), including risk of loss or damage, transfers to the Customer when the Handover and Commissioning Certificate is signed.

3. Trading Compliance. I accept full responsibility for all regulatory compliance including but not limited to: Food Standards Agency requirements, Local Authority and EHO registration and inspections (minimum 28 days before trading), Health and Safety Executive obligations, fire safety under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, HMRC tax and VAT obligations, and all site-specific licensing and venue conditions.

4. Equipment. I accept full responsibility for all third-party equipment including power compatibility verification against published unit specifications, delivery, installation, maintenance and removal. I acknowledge that Acme Hire accepts no liability for power incompatibility, equipment failure or any resulting damage to the unit or third parties.

5. Site Responsibility. I accept full responsibility for site preparation, power supply, utilities, ground conditions, access and all requirements as set out in the Hire Agreement. I confirm all required landlord consents and planning permissions have been obtained.

6. Support Charges. I acknowledge that Acme Hire support beyond initial unit setup or emergency response is chargeable at £50 per hour with a minimum charge of £100, and is only provided when pre-agreed in writing before the event.

7. Screen Artwork. I confirm that all screen artwork will be submitted through this Trader Briefing Pack (or otherwise agreed with Acme Hire) at least 7 days before delivery, and acknowledge that on-site screen changes incur a minimum fee of £100.

8. Insurance. I confirm that Public Liability (£10m minimum), Products Liability (£5m minimum) and, where staff are employed, Employers Liability insurance is in place; that the certificates have been uploaded with this pack; and that this cover will be maintained throughout the hire period.

9. Branding and Tracking. I confirm that Acme Hire branding, asset markings, GPS and tracking devices will not be removed, obscured or tampered with. No modifications or alterations will be made to the unit or equipment without Acme Hire's prior written consent.

10. Briefing Pack. I confirm I have read each section of the Acme Hire Trader Briefing Pack (AH-TBP-2026) in full and agree to operate in accordance with all instructions, requirements and responsibilities contained therein.
