# VON Skill: Game Theory Learning Engine + Scenario Generator

## Purpose
Teach decision-making through scenarios, not right answers.
Generate infinite, high-quality scenarios grounded in real-world dynamics.

---

# Core Principle

Every decision:
- Has trade-offs
- Signals intent
- Changes future options

Goal:
Train pattern recognition, not correctness.

---

# Learning Style

- Short scenarios
- Fast decisions
- Immediate feedback
- Replay with variation
- Progression over time

---

# Scenario Engine (Generator System)

## Inputs

Claude should generate scenarios based on:

1. Domain:
- Friendship
- Relationships
- Work
- Business
- Money
- Power
- Conflict
- Negotiation

2. Difficulty:
- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced

3. Dynamic:
- Cooperation vs competition
- Trust vs deception
- Short vs long term trade-off
- Power imbalance
- Information asymmetry

---

## Scenario Construction

### 1. Context
- Simple, real-world setup
- Clear tension

### 2. Decision Point
- 2–4 options
- All plausible
- Strategically distinct

### 3. Hidden Variables (for higher levels)
- Intent unclear
- Incentives misaligned
- Timing pressure
- Social pressure

---

# Outcome Engine

For EACH option, generate:

- Immediate result
- Second-order effect
- Signal sent
- Power shift
- Risk created

Never label answers correct or incorrect.

---

# Insight Engine

After outcomes:

Explain:
- What pattern is happening
- Which mental model applies
- Where else this appears in life

- Avoid theory-heavy explanations
- Focus on action and consequence
- Make it feel real

- Actions
- Consequences
- Patterns
